About the project

Review the journey, not just the destination.

Shotwright is a small, Playwright-powered harness for capturing the meaningful states in a product walkthrough. Its output is made for people: clear evidence that invites thoughtful review.

01

Script the story

Write walkthroughs with familiar Playwright locators, then name the exact moments worth discussing.

02

Keep the evidence

Each run gathers screenshots and optional recordings into a manifest that preserves the context of every state.

03

Invite human judgment

Captures make change visible without pretending that a pixel diff can replace a reviewer’s understanding.

Built for real workflows

The demo itself is captured by Shotwright. These pages and their walkthrough specs are both the showcase and the proving ground.
