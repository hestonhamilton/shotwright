## Sign-off

**Signed off by the orchestrator, with two recorded corrections to the plan's expectations.**

- **C4 retry retention.** The plan's V item expected the prior attempt's artifact and sidecar to *remain in the completed run* after a missing-source retry. That premise contradicts ADR 0021: manifest assembly publishes only each test's final attempt (`src/reporter.ts:39-40`) and removes stale captures. The W2 behaviour the plan actually pinned, opening the source before the retry unlink, is verified above (`RETRY_PRIOR_BYTES=PRIOR`, `RETRY_PRIOR_SIDECARS=1` immediately after the error). Its effect is limited to the remainder of the run, because the finished run truthfully reflects the final, failed attempt.
- **C4 memory.** Whole-run RSS sampling, which is dominated by the browser, does not show a reduction (path 478,904 KiB versus body 451,976 KiB). The claim that large artifacts do not cost their full size in memory rests on the chunked implementation and the unit test forbidding `readFileSync` on the path branch, not on a measurement.
- **C6 role.** `role="group"` is the approved W3 deviation from the plan's `role="region"`.

Release: the minor changeset resolves to 0.4.0. Publication is the owner's decision and has not happened.
